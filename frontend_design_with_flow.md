# 🎨 Frontend Design & Screen Flow (React.js)

## 🚀 Vision & Tech Stack
- **Framework**: React.js (Vite for fast builds)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS (with Glassmorphism & Modern Dark/Light mode support)
- **State Management**: Zustand (lightweight, fast)
- **Maps**: React Mapbox GL (for stunning 3D heatmaps)
- **Voice/Audio**: Web Speech API + integration with backend for vernacular languages
- **PWA**: Setup as a Progressive Web App so citizens can "Install" it on their phones without going to the Play Store.

---

## 📱 App Screen Flow (Detailed)

### 👥 Section 1: Citizen Side (Public App) - Focus: Zero Friction

**Screen 1: Splash / Landing Page**
- **UI/UX**: Beautiful local language greeting based on IP. "Apni Awaaz, Apne Neta Tak".
- **Action**: One-click language selection (Hindi, Marathi, English, etc.).
- **Wow Factor**: A live, subtly glowing ticker showing "X complaints resolved this week in your city".

**Screen 2: Quick Onboarding & Auth**
- **UI/UX**: Minimalist. 
- **Action**: Enter Phone Number -> OTP (Auto-read if possible).
- **Alternative**: "Continue as Guest" (Allows submission, but requires OTP only at the final step to avoid friction at the start).

**Screen 3: The "Report" Hub (The Most Important Screen)**
- **UI/UX**: 3 Massive, accessible buttons.
  - 🎙️ **Speak (Voice)**: Visual audio waves when the user speaks. "Boliye, aapki kya samasya hai?"
  - 📸 **Snap (Photo)**: Opens camera. "Problem ki photo kheenchiye."
  - ⌨️ **Type (Text)**: Standard text box.
- **Smart Features**: 
  - GPS automatically fetches Ward/Village name (requires location permission).
  - Auto-category suggestion based on the photo or voice input (e.g., photo of pothole -> UI auto-highlights "Roads").

**Screen 4: Community "Me Too" Feed (Citizen-to-Citizen Upvoting)**
- **UI/UX**: Instagram-like vertical scroll or Tinder-like swipe of *existing* issues in their 2km radius.
- **Action**: Citizen can click "Mujhe bhi yeh problem hai" (Upvote) instead of writing a new complaint.
- **Why?**: Reduces duplicate data entry and organically builds the "Severity Score".

**Screen 5: Submission Success & Tracking**
- **UI/UX**: Confetti animation.
- **Data Display**: "Aapki complaint AI ne scan kar li hai. Priority Score: 🔥 High (340 log aur pareshan hain)."
- **Tracking**: A visual timeline (Received -> AI Verified -> Forwarded to PWD -> Working -> Resolved).

---

### 👑 Section 2: MP / Admin Dashboard - Focus: Data Visualization & AI Insights

**Screen 1: Command Center (Main Dashboard)**
- **UI/UX**: Premium, dark-mode preferred, Bloomberg-terminal style but modern.
- **Top Widgets**: 
  - Total Active Issues vs Resolved.
  - AI Pulse: "Citizen Sentiment is currently 🔴 Frustrated in Ward 4."
- **Centerpiece**: Interactive 3D Heatmap. Red zones indicate high severity/volume issues. Hovering shows AI-summarized problem ("Water pipeline broken").

**Screen 2: AI Priority Report (The Winning View)**
- **UI/UX**: Ranked list, not of individual complaints, but of **AI Clusters**.
- **Item View**: 
  - **Rank #1**: "Road Condition in Andheri East" | 🔥 Severity: 9.8/10 | 👥 Mentions: 450 | 😠 Sentiment: Highly Negative.
- **Action**: Expanding the cluster shows the Gemini AI summary ("People are complaining about potholes causing accidents at night...") and lists the individual raw complaints underneath.

**Screen 3: Weekly Gemini AI Summary (The Report Card)**
- **UI/UX**: Looks like a beautifully formatted newsletter.
- **Content**: Auto-generated by AI. "Honorable MP, this week your constituency saw a 20% drop in water complaints, but a sudden spike in street-light issues in Sector 5. Recommend immediate action."
- **Action**: One-click "Generate PDF" or "Send to WhatsApp".

**Screen 4: Department Auto-Routing**
- **UI/UX**: Kanban board (Todo -> PWD, Todo -> Jal Board).
- **Action**: MP staff can just click "Approve AI Routing", and emails/alerts are automatically dispatched to the respective departments.

## ⭐ Advanced "Wow" Features (Frontend specific)

1. **Voice-Visualizer Feedback**: When rural citizens speak in their local language, the UI shows real-time translating text so they know the AI understood them correctly.
2. **Offline-First (Service Workers)**: If a citizen is in a village with no internet, they can still record a complaint. The app saves it locally and auto-syncs when they reach a network zone.
3. **Skeleton Loaders & Micro-animations**: No boring spinners. When AI is analyzing the complaint, show a scanning animation over the photo with text like "AI verifying issue severity..."
4. **WhatsApp Bot Integration Prompts**: A floating button saying "Prefer WhatsApp? Click here to report via WhatsApp." (Links to a Twilio/Meta WhatsApp bot).

## ♿ Accessibility (Crucial for Judging)
- **High Contrast Mode**: For visually impaired users.
- **Screen Reader Optimized**: Proper ARIA tags on all elements.
- **Icon-Heavy UI**: For users who cannot read, large recognizable icons (Tap drop for water, Bulb for electricity) instead of text.
